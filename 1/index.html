My Favorite Character: Indiana Jones

Indiana Jones is one of my favorite movie characters of all time. If you don't know him, he is an archaeologist and professor who adventures around the world in search of historical artifacts. One of his most iconic things about him is his western aesthetic. In his five movies that Harrison Ford stars in as Indiana Jones, the story takes us through adventures in the 1930s and 1940s. With the first movie being released in 1981, Indiana Jones goes on a quest to find the Ark of the Covenant and prevent it from falling into the hands of the Nazis.

I think outside of video games and sports, history is one of my favorite subjects to learn about and it really resonates with me and this character. He also has a very unique personality and is very witty and clever. He is also very brave and courageous, which are qualities I admire. As a child watching these movies made me more interested in history and the study of ancient civilizations.
