Welcome to the Viking Age

The Viking Age was a period of Scandinavian history that lasted from approximately the late eighth century to the early eleventh century.

Vikings were explorers, traders, craftspeople, warriors, and settlers. They traveled throughtout Europe and across the North Atlantic.

Timeline of the Viking Age

  1. 793 CE: The Raid on Lindisfarne
  2. 800s CE: Viking expansion and exploration
  3. 900s CE: Viking settlements in Iceland, Greenland, and North America
  4. 1000s CE: The Christianization of Scandinavia
  5. 1066 CE: The Battle of Stamford Bridge and the end of the Viking Age
