The Viking Age

Topic Description

The Viking Age was a period in European history that lasted from approximately 793 to 1066 CE. It is commonly associated with Norse people from areas that are now Denmark, Sweden, and Norway. The period is known for its exploration, trade, political changes, and raids throughtout Europe. The first well-known documented Viking raid occurrend at the monastery of Lindisfarne in England in 793 CE. Viking sailors eventually traveled across large parts of Europe and the North Atlantic, reaching places such as Iceland, Greenland, and even parts of North America.

Vikings are an interesting topic because they are often represented mainly as warriors and raiders, but their society was much more complicated. Most Vikings were ordinary farmers, while others were traders, craftspeople, and explorers. They had a rich culture that included art, mythology, and literature. The Viking Age also had a significant impact on the regions they interacted with, influencing local cultures, languages, and political structures. Viking society also experienced major religious and political changes, including the gradual conversion from Norse paganism to Christianity.

The main parts of the application will focus on exploration, daily life, religion, and Viking society. This will make the information hopefully more interactive than simply opening up a texbook or opening up a traditional webage.

App Description

The proposed web application will allow users to explore different parts of Viking history. The application will eventually contain four main areas: exploration, daily life, religion, and society. Users will be able to select one of these categories and then select a more specific topic within that category.

Two-step Interaction

The main interative portion of the application will use two steps. First, the user will choose one of four main categories. After making that choice, the user will be presented with four related topics.

  1. Choose a main Viking Age Category.
  2. Select a specific topic within the chosen category.

The four main categories will be Exploration, Daily Life, Religion, and Society. For example, a user who chooses Exploration could then choose Iceland, Greenland, North America, or Eastern Europe. The application would then display the information they chose.

Additional Pages

The completed application will also contain a home page, a page with additonal Viking Age information, and a Contact Us page. The additional information page could contain a timeline of the Viking Age and other historical info. The Contact Us page will allow users to submit their name and the contact info they wish to share.
